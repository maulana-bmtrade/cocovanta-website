EDITORIAL STATEMENT

Strategic Sourcing & Export Coordination Specialist

COCOVANTA connects global buyers with Indonesian cocopeat and coconut coir products through sourcing, supplier coordination, and export support for horticulture and agricultural markets.

Operating as an independent export desk based in Indonesia, we bridge the gap between international buyers and potential regional coir processors, subject to due diligence. We do not operate manufacturing plants; instead, we prioritize rigorous parameter verification, sample coordination, and transparent communication to work toward clear delivery alignment.

OUR MISSION

Key Commitments to International Importers

  • Supplier Sourcing & Due Diligence: Identifying potential Indonesian processing partners and checking relevant business, product and operational information before presenting an offer.
  • Specification Verification: Coordinating confirmation of EC, pH and expansion yield against supplier documents, samples and agreed inspection arrangements.
  • Sample Dispatch Coordination: Facilitating laboratory testing samples so clients can verify technical parameters prior to ordering.
  • Transparent Export Communication: Providing straightforward updates regarding production lead times, port logistics, and documentation.
  • Export Compliance Handling: Supporting commercial documentation including Phytosanitary Certificates and Certificates of Origin.
