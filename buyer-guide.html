Information that helps us assess your request

1. Product requirements

  • Product type and intended application
  • Format: loose, compressed block, grow bag, chips or fiber
  • Target EC, pH, moisture, particle size or fiber length, if relevant
  • Packaging, dimensions and labeling requirements

2. Commercial requirements

  • Trial quantity and expected repeat volume
  • Destination country and discharge port
  • Preferred Incoterm (e.g. FOB, CFR or CIF)
  • Target shipment window and payment expectations

3. Evidence to request

  • Current batch specification and test report, if available
  • Recent product and packaging photographs
  • Sample availability, sample quantity and courier cost
  • Supplier identity and facility details before commitment

4. Before placing an order

  • Confirm supplier identity and authority to sell
  • Agree final specifications in writing
  • Verify price, quantity, packing, lead time and Incoterm
  • Review applicable export and destination requirements

How COCOVANTA handles an inquiry

  1. 01 — Requirement review. We review the product, quantity, destination and target parameters you provide.
  2. 02 — Sourcing check. We approach potential Indonesian suppliers and assess whether they can address the request. Supplier identity, availability and capability must be confirmed case by case.
  3. 03 — Evidence and quotation. Where available, we request supplier-backed specifications, photos, sample details and commercial terms for your review.
  4. 04 — Export coordination. If both parties agree to proceed, the parties clarify contracting, payment, inspection, shipping and documentation responsibilities before any commitment.

COCOVANTA is an independent sourcing and export coordination desk, not a manufacturer. This website does not represent that a supplier, product batch, certificate, sample, shipment or export capability has already been verified. Do not treat indicative or target parameters as a confirmed offer.
